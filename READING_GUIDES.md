# ERL Reading Guides

A bilingual, work-by-work guide to explanatory articles for the papers and methods in [Awesome Evolutionary Reinforcement Learning](README.md). This repository is the official companion to [*Bridging Evolutionary Algorithms and Reinforcement Learning: A Comprehensive Survey on Hybrid Algorithms*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.11963).

> [!NOTE]
> Every distinct work in the main collection was searched independently in Chinese and English on 29 Aug 2026. Links are included only when the article provides a substantive, work-specific explanation. `—` means that no reliable written explanation passed review; it does not mean the paper or code is unavailable.

**Coverage:** 123 distinct works reviewed · 10 Chinese guides · 17 English guides · 19 works with at least one verified guide.

## Curation policy

- Prefer author, research-lab, university, and reputable technical-publisher explanations.
- Exclude paper landing pages, ordinary repository READMEs, abstract mirrors, scraped summaries, generic background material, and video-only resources.
- Official project pages are included only when they contain substantial method, experiment, or implementation explanations beyond an abstract.
- Recheck links before relying on them for teaching or long-term archival use.
